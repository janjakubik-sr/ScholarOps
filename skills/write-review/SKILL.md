# Write Review

## Workflow

1. Confirm the journal and manuscript type from supplied context. If the journal is unknown and its standards materially affect the review, ask for it; otherwise draft a journal-neutral review and label that limitation.
2. Search the journal's official current reviewer instructions, editorial criteria, reporting requirements, scope, and review format. Prefer official journal or publisher pages and record the URLs and access date. Do not substitute generic advice when journal-specific guidance is available.
3. Read the completed artifacts from reference control, data/statistics audit, internal-logic audit, and independent evidence audit. Do not reopen the analysis unless an artifact is missing, contradictory, or clearly unsupported.
4. Deduplicate findings and rank them by impact on validity, interpretation, reproducibility, ethics, and journal fit. Distinguish essential revisions from optional improvements.
5. Draft a concise review:
   - brief neutral summary of the manuscript and contribution
   - overall assessment with strengths and principal limitations
   - numbered major comments, each stating the issue, why it matters, and a feasible requested action
   - numbered minor comments limited to useful corrections
   - confidential editor comments only if requested or required, and never leak them into author comments
6. Apply the journal's terminology and required structure. Avoid requesting experiments that are merely interesting; request new work only when necessary to support a central claim or satisfy an explicit standard.
7. Balance criticism with specific strengths. Keep tone professional, respectful, non-dismissive, and focused on the work rather than the authors.
8. Before finalizing, verify every review comment against the audit artifacts and manuscript location. Remove unsupported, duplicated, vague, or disproportionate criticism.

## Output Contract

- **Journal standards used:** official sources and the constraints applied.
- **Review to authors:** summary, overall assessment, major comments, minor comments.
- **Optional confidential comments to editor:** only when requested.
- **Traceability appendix:** map each major comment to the source audit artifact and manuscript location; keep separate from the clean review unless the user asks to include it.

## Boundaries

- Do not invent journal requirements, manuscript facts, reviewer expertise, or audit findings.
- Do not reveal author identities, speculate about misconduct, or make accusations without direct evidence.
- Do not let polished prose conceal unresolved validity problems; state blockers plainly.
